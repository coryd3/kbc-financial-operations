# Reimbursement Implementation Draft

Status: Needs Finance Review

## Relationship To The Approved Recommendation

Finance Committee approved one combined [Kingsville Baptist Church Spending Policy](spending-authority-policy.md) recommendation on July 19, 2026. That recommendation includes a 15-day deadline and requires a receipt, ministry purpose, budget category, and required approvals.

This page was **not separately approved** by Finance Committee. It proposes implementation details for review and must not be represented as part of the July 19 recommendation unless Finance Committee adopts those details through a later action.

## Purpose

This draft describes how church-related expenses could be reimbursed while maintaining prior approval, documentation, accountability, and review.

## Scope

This draft applies to reimbursement requests from staff, volunteers, ministry leaders, committee members, and others who purchase authorized items for KBC ministry or operations.

## Proposed General Rules

- Reimbursements must be for a valid church purpose.
- The expense must follow the approved budget and approval requirements in the combined Spending Policy.
- Reimbursement after a purchase does not cure an expense that required pre-approval.
- Requests must be submitted within 15 days of the expense.
- The requester may not be the only approver.
- The Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator may verify and process a complete request but does not gain approval authority merely by processing it.
- Reimbursement activity and exceptions should be included in monthly Finance Committee review.

## Proposed Required Documentation

In addition to the items in the approved recommendation, a complete request should identify:

- Requester name.
- Date of purchase.
- Vendor.
- Amount.
- Church or ministry purpose.
- Ministry, fund, or budget category.
- Receipt or invoice.
- Evidence of the required approval.

## Proposed Exceptions

The July 19 recommendation does not say what happens when a request is late, a receipt is missing, or required approval was not obtained before purchase.

A missing-receipt request could require:

- A signed explanation stating why the receipt is unavailable.
- The vendor, date, amount, church purpose, and budget category.
- Available secondary evidence, such as a vendor reprint, order confirmation, packing slip, or card record.
- Independent approval before payment.

Repeated late submissions, missing receipts, or purchases made without approval should be reported to Finance Committee.

## Proposed Review Process

1. The requester submits the request, documentation, and evidence of required approval.
2. The assigned approver confirms the church purpose, budget category, timing, and compliance with the combined Spending Policy.
3. The Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator verifies completeness and prepares the reimbursement if assigned.
4. The Treasurer / Financial Officer or another independent reviewer checks documentation and policy compliance.
5. Finance Committee reviews reimbursement totals, late requests, missing receipts, unusual items, and other exceptions monthly.

Needs Finance Review: Finance Committee should decide approver roles, late-submission exceptions, missing-receipt standards, no-preapproval handling, payment release, record retention, and monthly reporting before using this implementation draft.
