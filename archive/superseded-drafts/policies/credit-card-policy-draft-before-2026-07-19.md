# Card And Controlled Payment Implementation Draft

Status: Needs Finance Review

## Relationship To The Approved Recommendation

Finance Committee approved one combined [Kingsville Baptist Church Spending Policy](spending-authority-policy.md) recommendation on July 19, 2026. Its card section allows issuance as deemed necessary, requires limits and receipts, requires monthly statement review, prohibits personal purchases and sharing, and makes sharing grounds for card cancellation and immediate forfeiture of custody.

This page was **not separately approved** by Finance Committee. It proposes implementation details for review and must not be represented as part of the July 19 recommendation unless Finance Committee adopts those details through a later action.

## Purpose

This draft proposes an accountable approach to physical cards, virtual cards, online payment credentials, and other controlled payment access.

## Proposed Access Controls

- Issue cards or controlled payment access only to specifically approved individuals.
- Record each approved user, purpose, account limit, transaction limit, and review date.
- Do not use shared group credentials.
- Do not treat possession of a card or a card limit as spending authority.
- Give the Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator payment access only when specifically approved.

## Proposed User Responsibilities

Each approved user should:

- Use payment access only for a valid church purpose.
- Stay within the approved budget, payment limit, and combined Spending Policy.
- Obtain required pre-approval.
- Protect cards, numbers, credentials, and other payment access.
- Never share payment access.
- Never use church payment access for personal purchases.
- Report lost, stolen, or compromised access immediately.
- Submit complete transaction documentation promptly.

## Proposed Transaction Documentation

Each transaction should identify:

- Vendor.
- Date.
- Amount.
- Church or ministry purpose.
- Ministry, fund, or budget category.
- Receipt or invoice.
- Evidence of required approval.

## Proposed Monthly Review

An independent reviewer should compare documentation to each card or payment statement. Monthly Finance Committee review should include:

- Missing receipts or incomplete records.
- Unusual, disputed, over-budget, or outside-budget transactions.
- Personal, unclear, or unattributed charges.
- Spending-limit or approval concerns.
- Lost, stolen, or compromised access.
- Policy exceptions and corrective actions.

## Proposed Incident Response

The July 19 recommendation requires card cancellation and immediate forfeiture of custody when a card or its information is shared. Finance Committee should also define:

- Who reports and contacts the issuer about lost, stolen, or compromised access.
- Who investigates disputed or unclear charges.
- When repayment, training, suspension, or other corrective action applies.
- How access is removed when a cardholder changes or leaves a role.
- What records are retained.

Needs Finance Review: Finance Committee should confirm eligibility, issuance, individual limits, documentation, statement review, incident response, corrective action, and record retention before using this implementation draft.
