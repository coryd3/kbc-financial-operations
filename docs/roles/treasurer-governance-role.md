# Kingsville Baptist Church

# Treasurer / Financial Officer - Role Description

Status: Current

Rev. September 2026

## Position Summary

The Treasurer / Financial Officer is a church officer who helps ensure that Kingsville Baptist Church's financial records, reports, controls, and accountability practices are maintained faithfully and transparently.

The Treasurer provides financial oversight, works closely with the Finance Committee, and keeps the church informed as required. Routine bookkeeping may be performed by the same person under the church's preferred staffing arrangement or by a separate Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator.

The Treasurer does not replace the authority of the church or the responsibilities assigned to the Finance Committee and other governing bodies.

## Coordinated Staffing Arrangement

Kingsville Baptist Church's preferred staffing arrangement is to select one qualified person to serve as Treasurer and perform the paid Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator duties described in the companion role description. The combined service arrangement is compensated under terms approved separately by the church.

The Treasurer remains a church officer recommended by the Nominating Committee and selected by the church in accordance with the Constitution and Bylaws. Compensation does not change the Treasurer's accountability to the church or the authority of the church, Finance Committee, or other governing bodies.

If the combined arrangement cannot be filled, the church may employ a separate Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator and fill the Treasurer office separately as a volunteer position.

## Role Distinction and Review

The Treasurer is responsible for governance, oversight, reporting, and accountability. The Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator is responsible for routine financial operations and preparation of records, reconciliations, and reports.

When one person performs both roles, that person carries both sets of responsibilities but must still follow the controls and approval requirements that apply to each task. Work that requires review must be reviewed by the Finance Committee or another reviewer designated under approved church policy; the preparer should not be the only reviewer.

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Financial Oversight & Accountability

- Serve as the church's financial officer and remain accountable to the church under its governing documents.
- Work with the Finance Committee to support faithful stewardship and shared financial accountability.
- Monitor compliance with approved budgets, financial policies, spending authority, and documentation requirements.
- Confirm that financial records are organized, protected, and available to authorized reviewers.
- Communicate significant discrepancies, policy exceptions, control concerns, or decisions needing church action.

### 2. Review & Reporting

- Review monthly bank reconciliations, financial reports, and supporting documentation when the roles are held by separate individuals.
- When one person performs both roles, ensure that another reviewer designated by the Finance Committee or approved church policy completes the required review.
- Review budget-to-actual results, significant variances, designated or restricted fund balances, and material exceptions.
- Present or ensure presentation of financial information to the Finance Committee, church leadership, and church business meetings as required.
- Confirm that unresolved items are referred to the Finance Committee or other appropriate church body.

### 3. Finance Committee Coordination

- Work closely with the Finance Committee on budgets, reports, financial controls, and policy implementation.
- Help carry out approved Finance Committee direction within the authority granted by the church.
- Support the Finance Committee's review of spending, reimbursements, deposits, payment activity, payroll summaries, and other financial matters.
- Coordinate with the Personnel Committee when a financial matter affects employment, evaluation, or compensation administration.

### 4. Audit, Review & Continuity

- Support annual financial reviews, audits, or outside accounting assistance authorized by the church.
- Ensure that requested records and explanations are provided by the appropriate preparer or recordkeeper.
- Help maintain shared procedures and transferable knowledge so the church does not depend on one person alone.
- Serve as the church liaison with outside payroll or accounting providers when assigned, while allowing routine communication to be delegated with oversight.

### 5. Coordination with the Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator

- When the roles are held by separate individuals, provide oversight without taking over routine bookkeeping work.
- Review reports, reconciliations, exceptions, and supporting documentation prepared by the Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator.
- Help resolve questions involving policy, authorization, unusual transactions, or church reporting.
- Support clear deadlines, complete records, and respectful communication among the Bookkeeper, Finance Committee, and authorized reviewers.

## Qualifications

The Treasurer / Financial Officer should:

- Be a born-again, baptized believer in Jesus Christ.
- Be a member of Kingsville Baptist Church in good standing and eligible to serve as a church officer.
- Demonstrate integrity, confidentiality, faithfulness, and sound judgment in financial matters.
- Be able to review financial reports, ask clarifying questions, and communicate financial information clearly.
- Be willing to work cooperatively with the Finance Committee, Personnel Committee, Pastor, Deacons, Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator, and authorized outside professionals.
- Be committed to serving in harmony with the Constitution and Bylaws, approved church policies, and church decisions.
- Complete any background check, bonding, confidentiality, or onboarding requirements established for the role.

## Accountability & Selection

- The Treasurer is recommended by the Nominating Committee and must receive the church vote required by the Constitution and Bylaws.
- The Treasurer remains accountable to the church and works closely with the Finance Committee.
- The Treasurer serves a one-year term and may be renominated and approved in accordance with the Constitution and Bylaws.
- When the same person performs the paid Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator duties, compensation does not replace or bypass the church-officer selection process.
- Removal or replacement of the Treasurer follows the Constitution and Bylaws and applicable church decisions.

## Expectations for Character & Service

- Live above reproach and demonstrate honesty, discretion, and care in financial dealings.
- Model Christian stewardship through faithful service and example.
- Be dependable, consistent, accurate, and timely.
- Communicate calmly and respectfully with church members, leaders, staff, volunteers, and outside professionals.
- Protect confidential donor, payroll, banking, personnel, and financial information.

## Terms of Service

The Treasurer / Financial Officer serves as a church officer for the term and under the selection, accountability, and removal provisions established by the Kingsville Baptist Church Constitution and Bylaws. When one person also performs the paid Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator duties, the employment arrangement and compensation for those operational duties are administered separately under the church's approved authority and do not alter the Treasurer's officer responsibilities.
