## Spending Approval Levels

| Spending Amount | Approval Required |
| --- | --- |
| Up to `$250` | Pastor can approve |
| `$251-$1,000` | Pastor & the Finance Committee Chair's approval is required |
| `$1,001-$3,000` | Finance Committee approval required |
| More than `$3,000` | Church approval at a regular or special-called business meeting |

## General Spending Rules

- All spending must fit within the approved annual budget.
- Approved budgeted items and designated funds do not automatically authorize spending; approval levels still apply.
- Approval authority does not authorize spending outside the approved budget unless separately approved by the Finance Committee or the Church.
- No individual may commit church funds without the appropriate prior approval.
- Personal purchases may never be made with church funds.

## Quotes And Competitive Bids

- Under `$1,000`: reasonable pricing expected.
- `$1,000-$3,000`: obtain at least two quotes when practical.
- More than `$3,000`: obtain at least three written quotes unless only one qualified vendor exists.
- Purchases exceeding `$25,000` require approval by a two-thirds vote of the members present at a special-called business meeting.
  - A quorum of 30% of the current year's average Sunday Morning attendance is required for these purchases.

## Contract Authority

- No employee, volunteer, or committee may sign contracts on behalf of the Church unless authorized by the Church or the Finance Committee.

## Emergency Purchases

- Emergency spending to protect people, prevent major property damage, or restore essential services is granted to the Deacons, Building and Grounds Committee, and the Finance Committee. This spending should be communicated to the Church promptly.

## Reimbursements

- Reimbursement requests must include receipts, the ministry purpose, the applicable budget category, all required approvals, and be submitted within *15 days* of the purchase.

## Church Credit/Debit Cards

- The church may issue cards to those deemed necessary, with spending limits set by the church or the Finance Committee.
- All purchases require a receipt.
- Credit and debit card statements shall be reviewed monthly by the Finance Committee or its designee.
- Personal purchases are prohibited with church funds.
- Credit/Debit cards and card information **cannot** be given to anyone other than the approved cardholder.
  - Violation of this will result in the card being canceled and immediate forfeiture of card custody.

## Conflict Of Interest Disclosure

- Any church officer, committee member, employee, or volunteer with a financial interest in a proposed purchase shall disclose that interest and abstain from participating in the approval of that purchase.

## Tax Exemption

- The church is a 501c3 and is thus exempt from sales tax in the state of Missouri.
- All purchasers should attempt to make purchases with this benefit.
  - This is best practice, but it is understood that this is not always possible.
