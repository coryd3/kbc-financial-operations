# Personnel Committee Recommendation — Ministry And Staff Evaluation Process

Status: Draft

Rev. August 2026

## Purpose

Help Kingsville Baptist Church support ministry directors and paid employees with clear, encouraging, and manageable annual feedback. The goal is growth, clarity, and healthy accountability — not corporate HR complexity.

## Proposed Solution

Start with a **simple annual evaluation cycle** built around three short pieces:

1. **Self-evaluation** completed by the person being reviewed
2. **Peer / ministry input** from a small number of people who serve with them
3. **Personnel Committee summary** shared in a private one-on-one conversation

Keep the first year **comment-based**, not rating-based. Ratings can be added later if the church finds them helpful.

## Who Is Covered

- Ministry directors
- Paid employees / staff roles overseen by Personnel Committee

Elected officers remain governed by bylaws and church practice. If an elected role also needs feedback, Personnel should confirm the proper path before treating it the same as staff evaluation.

## Recommended Process

### Step 1 — Self-evaluation

The person completes a short form covering:

- Role clarity: What does this role require right now?
- Strengths and accomplishments from the past year
- Challenges or areas for growth
- Support needed from the church, Pastor, or committees
- Goals for the coming year

Estimated time: 20–30 minutes.

### Step 2 — Peer / ministry input

The person nominates **2–3 people** who know their ministry or work well.

Personnel Committee:

- Reviews the nominations
- Approves those reviewers and may add **1 additional reviewer** when needed
- Sends a short input form focused on:
  - Areas the person is doing well
  - Areas for growth or improvement
  - Overall comments / encouragement

No numeric ratings in year one.

Estimated time for each reviewer: 10–15 minutes.

### Step 3 — Committee summary and one-on-one

Personnel Committee:

- Reviews the self-evaluation and all input forms
- Prepares one short written summary
- Meets one-on-one with the person to share feedback, affirm strengths, and agree on 1–3 growth goals

The written summary stays confidential with Personnel, the Pastor as appropriate, and the person reviewed.

## Why This Fits KBC

| Church need | How this helps |
| --- | --- |
| Support growth without overload | Short forms; comment-based; limited reviewers |
| Clear expectations | Ties feedback to the person's actual role |
| Fair and encouraging process | Self-reflection + peer input + private conversation |
| Volunteer-friendly | Manageable for a part-time Personnel Committee |
| Room to improve later | Can add ratings or more structure after the first cycle |

## What We Are Not Doing In Year One

- Forced ranking or scorecards
- Public review in business meeting
- Large multi-page corporate appraisal packets
- Evaluating every volunteer role at once

## Suggested Cadence

- **Once per year**, ideally on a shared calendar so ministry directors and paid staff know when to expect it
- Allow about **4–6 weeks** from kickoff to completed one-on-one conversations
