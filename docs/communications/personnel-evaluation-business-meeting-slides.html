What We Are Working On

Establishing simple standard operating procedures

One priority: a clear, encouraging annual evaluation process for ministry directors and paid employees.
  • Help people grow in their calling and role
  • Clarify expectations for the church and for each role
  • Provide support without creating heavy HR paperwork
2 / 3

Proposed Process

1. Self-evaluation — strengths, challenges, support needed, and goals
2. Peer / ministry input — person nominates 2–3 people; Personnel may add one more
Short form: doing well · areas to grow · overall comments
3. One-on-one feedback — Personnel summarizes input and agrees on 1–3 growth goals

Year one stays simple: comments, not ratings. Manageable for a volunteer committee.
