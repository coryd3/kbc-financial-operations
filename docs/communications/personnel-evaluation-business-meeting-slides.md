# Personnel Committee Update
## Building Healthy Support For Ministry Leaders And Staff

Kingsville Baptist Church Business Meeting

---

# What We Are Working On

Personnel Committee is establishing simple **standard operating procedures**.

One priority:

> A clear, encouraging annual evaluation process for **ministry directors** and **paid employees**

**Why?**

- Help people grow in their calling and role
- Clarify expectations
- Provide support without creating heavy HR paperwork

---

# Proposed Process

**1. Self-evaluation**  
Each person reflects on strengths, challenges, support needed, and goals.

**2. Peer / ministry input**  
The person nominates 2–3 people; Personnel may add one more.  
Short form: *doing well* · *areas to grow* · *overall comments*

**3. One-on-one feedback**  
Personnel brings the input together and shares it privately, with 1–3 growth goals.

**Year one stays simple:** comments, not ratings. Manageable for a volunteer committee.
